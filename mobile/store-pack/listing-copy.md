## Full description (canonical, both stores, Apple max 4,000 characters)

Audience order: businesses first, because businesses are the paying side and the category buyer searching the store, and creators are told in the second breath that joining is free.

---

Magpie is a local influencer marketing platform. Local businesses book nearby creators, and creators are ranked by local reach: how many of their followers actually live in the business's city, shown before anyone books. Follower count is not the headline. Local reach is.

For businesses:
Browse creators in your market, ranked by the share of their audience that lives near you, with each creator's rate and past visit record on their card. Book a comped visit (your meal, service, or experience, at your cost) or a paid booking at the creator's own listed rate. Every visit is tracked in the app in three steps: Checked in, Posts live, and Redeemed, so you can see who showed up, what was posted, and who came in from the post. To redeem a visit, the creator shows their code in the app and your staff scan or enter it at the counter.

For creators:
Join free. You never pay to join, to be listed, or to get booked. Set your own rate for paid bookings and keep 97% of it. The only creator cost is 3% on a paid booking, taken from the payout. Comped visits carry no fee at all. Paid money is held until your post is live. Your profile shows businesses your local reach first, which is how smaller local followings get booked ahead of larger distant ones.

How a visit works:
A business posts an offer or books you directly. You visit, the business redeems your code, and you post honestly, with the goal of posting within 48 hours of the visit. Honest posts only: no business can require praise.

Founding, city by city:
Magpie is opening market by market. Founding creators and founding businesses join now in their city, and booking opens in each market as its roster is ready. Founding business plans are available; current plan details and prices are on magpielocal.com, not listed here, so this page never goes stale.

Sign in or apply from the first screen. You must be 18 or older to use Magpie.
